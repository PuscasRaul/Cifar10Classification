* Reading list
** Feature extraction
1. [core] Dalal & Triggs, "Histograms of Oriented Gradients for Human Detection" (CVPR 2005)
2. [core] Ojala, Pietikäinen & Mäenpää, "Multiresolution Gray-Scale and Rotation Invariant Texture Classification with Local Binary Patterns" (TPAMI 2002)
3. [optional] Oliva & Torralba, "Modeling the Shape of the Scene: A Holistic Representation of the Spatial Envelope" (IJCV 2001), the GIST paper
4. [optional] Csurka et al., "Visual Categorization with Bags of Keypoints" (2004)
5. [optional] Lowe, "Distinctive Image Features from Scale-Invariant Keypoints" (IJCV 2004), for SIFT background

** Unsupervised and random feature learning
1. [core] Coates, Ng & Lee, "An Analysis of Single-Layer Networks in Unsupervised Feature Learning" (AISTATS 2011)
2. [core] Coates & Ng, "The Importance of Encoding Versus Training with Sparse Coding and Vector Quantization" (ICML 2011)
3. [core] Jarrett et al., "What is the Best Multi-Stage Architecture for Object Recognition?" (ICCV 2009)
4. [core] Saxe et al., "On Random Weights and Unsupervised Feature Learning" (ICML 2011)
5. [optional] Coates & Ng, "Learning Feature Representations with K-means" (2012), practical details on whitening and k-means

** Scattering transforms
1. [core] Bruna & Mallat, "Invariant Scattering Convolution Networks" (TPAMI 2013)
2. [core] Oyallon & Mallat, "Deep Roto-Translation Scattering for Object Classification" (CVPR 2015)
3. [optional] Oyallon et al., "Scattering Networks for Hybrid Representation Learning" (TPAMI 2018)
** Trees on image features
1. [core] Bosch, Zisserman & Muñoz, "Image Classification Using Random Forests and Ferns" (ICCV 2007). It's the most direct precedent for your project.
2. [optional] Frosst & Hinton, "Distilling a Neural Network Into a Soft Decision Tree" (2017)

** Rigor and methodology
1. [core] Demšar, "Statistical Comparisons of Classifiers over Multiple Data Sets" (JMLR 2006)
2. [core] Dietterich, "Approximate Statistical Tests for Comparing Supervised Classification Learning Algorithms" (Neural Computation 1998)
3. [core] Bergstra & Bengio, "Random Search for Hyper-Parameter Optimization" (JMLR 2012)
4. [core] Bouthillier et al., "Accounting for Variance in Machine Learning Benchmarks" (MLSys 2021)
5. [core] Recht et al., "Do CIFAR-10 Classifiers Generalize to CIFAR-10?" (2018). It gives you CIFAR-10.1 as an extra test set.
6. [optional] Fernández-Delgado et al., "Do We Need Hundreds of Classifiers to Solve Real World Classification Problems?" (JMLR 2014)

** From earlier (trees, ensembles, PCA, boosting)
Breiman (CART 1984, RF 2001), Quinlan (ID3/C4.5), Geurts (Extra-Trees), Rodríguez (Rotation Forest), Bengio et al. 2010, Friedman 2001, XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost, Halko et al. 2011, Murthy (OC1), Tomita (SPORF), and ESL Ch. 9, 10, 15.
